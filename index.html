Capabilities

Everything your ranch needs

From a small goat herd to a multi-species operation, Arpent adapts to how you work.

🐄

Multi-Species Support

Manage cattle, sheep, goats, hogs, and poultry in a single system. Species-specific fields, units, and workflows out of the box.

🌾

Pasture Tracking

Map your pastures, track rotations, and monitor grazing pressure. Know which fields are resting, active, or ready for the next move.

💉

Health Logs

Record treatments, vaccinations, and withdrawal dates. Stay compliant and never lose a health record again.

📈

Sell Timing (Encan)

Model market scenarios, target weights, and cost-of-gain to find the optimal time to sell each group.

🗺️

Grazing Planning (Estive)

Conservative grazing-capacity calculations and rotation plans. Avoid overgrazing with science-backed stocking rates.

📴

Offline-Capable

No cell service? No problem. Arpent works offline and syncs when you're back in range. Built for the field, not the office.
